---
name: Diary quote localization
description: Preserve the source quote selected by the user's rotation when localizing the diary's dynamic motivation quotes.
---

Translate the quote's template fragments rather than replacing the rotation with a smaller set of sample quotes.

**Why:** Substituting sample quotes can make the displayed text no longer correspond to the original quote selected by its rotation index.

**How to apply:** When localizing rotating quotes, preserve the source item and index; translate its reusable fragments or complete source-matched entries.
